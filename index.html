FAQ

Quick answers

Can you spot-reduce belly fat with ab exercises?

No. Controlled studies show ab training alone does not significantly reduce belly fat. Ab work builds the muscle; an overall calorie deficit reveals it. AB6 plans combine both.

What body fat percentage do you need to see abs?

As a rule of thumb, most men see a clear six-pack around 10–12% body fat and most women around 16–19%. Genetics, ab thickness and where you store fat shift this a few points either way.

How often should I train abs?

Two to four focused core sessions per week is enough for most people. Treat abs like any other muscle: progressive overload, good form and recovery.

# AB6.com — Concept Decision and Competitor Research

## 1. Concept decision

**Domain reading:** "AB6" is read as **"Ab Six"**, meaning six-pack abs. The domain is 3 characters and describes the topic on sight, which suits a single-niche authority site.

| Candidate | Search demand | Money per visitor | Fit with "AB6" | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Six-pack abs / core training hub** | Very high. Ab-workout keywords run in the hundreds of thousands (see below) | High: coaching leads, fitness ads, gear affiliates, sponsors | Direct ("Ab Six") | **Chosen** |
| Vitamin B6 ("a B6") | Medium | Medium (supplement affiliates) | Weak | Rejected |
| Alberta Bill 6 (farm safety law) | Very low | Low | Weak | Rejected |
| A/B testing tool | Medium | High (SaaS) | Weak; the "6" doesn't fit | Rejected |

## 2. Market data (gathered October 2026)

- Roughly $10–28 on premium ad networks, with the top 10% above $40 ([Earnifyhub](https://earnifyhub.com/blog/blogging/blog-display-ad-rpm-by-niche-2026)).

- CPC: $6.17
- Conversion rate: 6.94%

- Personal-training leads cost $22–75 on Facebook (average about $32–52) ([Webtonic](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/fitness-facebook-ads-statistics)).

**YouTube RPM, fitness:** about $3–10 ([SocialCal](https://www.socialcal.app/blog/how-much-does-youtube-pay-for-1000-views)).

## 3. Trademark check

- **AB6IX** is a K-pop group run by the agency Brand New Music. The name stands for "Absolute Six", and the group has been on hiatus since May 2026. It is an entertainment brand, not a fitness one, but the name is close. **Do not use its name, imagery or styling.**
- **US filings:** "AB6000" is filed for e-cigarettes. AB6 Holdings LLC holds festival and record-label marks. Neither covers "AB6" for fitness.
- **Action:** run USPTO, CIPO and EUIPO searches before any trademark filing.
- A non-affiliation notice appears in the site footer and on `disclaimer.html`.

## 5. Features shipped (mapped to the research)

**Tools**
- Body-fat, macro and abs-timeline calculators (calculator.net, Precision Nutrition, TDEEcalculator)
- Exercise library with an interactive muscle map (MuscleWiki, ACE, ExRx)
- Workout generator with a voice timer (MuscleWiki, Fitness Blender, Freeletics)
- Core strength test (DAREBEE fitness test)

**Engagement**
- 6-week challenge with a tracker and prize-draw entries (Sweat, Chloe Ting, Shape)
- Workout of the day (DAREBEE)

**Lead generation**
- Quiz funnel that shows the plan summary free and asks for email to unlock the full plan (Precision Nutrition, Athlean-X)
- Coach-match application plus a coach-network page for trainers (Nerd Fitness, NASM, ISSA, Thumbtack)
- Exit-intent lead magnet (Legion, Bodybuilding.com)

**Monetisation**
- AdSense, a YouTube hub, a gear affiliate guide with disclosure (Garage Gym Reviews), sponsorship packages, and donations (DAREBEE)

**Community**
- Contests with official rules, careers page, legal pages

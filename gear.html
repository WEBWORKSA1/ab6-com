Home ab gear guide

You need nothing to start. These are the few pieces that unlock the hardest, most effective core exercises.

Ab wheel (wide, dual wheel)

The best value-for-money tool in core training. Wide dual wheels are more stable for beginners. Look for a thick knee pad and grippy handles.

Doorway pull-up bar

Unlocks hanging knee/leg raises — the best lower-ab progression. Check door-frame width and the weight rating.

Resistance band set with anchor

For Pallof presses and woodchops — anti-rotation work that bodyweight can't easily replace.

Exercise mat (10–15 mm)

Comfort for floor work. Thicker for comfort, thinner for stability during planks.
